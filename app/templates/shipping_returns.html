Fulfillment

Black Metal Buddha uses Printful for print-on-demand production and shipping. We submit an order for fulfillment only after Square confirms payment.

Shipping

We ship only to the United States and Canada. Shipping is charged separately using the current Printful quote for your delivery address.

Available shipping methods and rates are requested from Printful for your delivery address during checkout. Delivery dates are estimates rather than guarantees. Tracking appears on your order-status page when supplied by the carrier.

Damaged, defective, or misprinted

Contact us within 30 days of delivery if an item arrives damaged, defective, misprinted, or materially different from the item ordered. We may ask for clear photographs so the fulfillment claim can be reviewed. Eligible claims will be resolved with a replacement or refund as appropriate.

Lost in transit

Please report a package that appears lost within 30 days after its estimated delivery date. We will review the carrier and fulfillment record and arrange the appropriate remedy when the shipment qualifies as lost.

Size, color, and change of mind

Products are made to order, so we generally do not accept returns or exchanges for buyer's remorse, ordering the wrong size, or choosing the wrong color. This does not limit rights that cannot legally be waived in your jurisdiction.

Address problems

Please review your shipping address carefully. Orders returned because of an incomplete or incorrect address may require a new shipping charge before they can be sent again.
