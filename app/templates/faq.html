Who prints and ships the shirts?

Printful handles automated print-on-demand production and shipment after Black Metal Buddha verifies a completed Square payment.

How are payments handled?

Card and other enabled fiat checkout methods are processed on Square's hosted checkout. Black Metal Buddha does not handle raw card details.

What about Bitcoin and Lightning?

Lightning remains intentionally deferred until Square exposes an official online API flow that can accept Lightning and automatically settle the merchant side to fiat without manual conversion.

Can I return a shirt because I ordered the wrong size?

These are made-to-order products, so size/color mistakes and change-of-mind returns generally are not accepted. Damaged, defective, misprinted, incorrect, or qualifying lost orders are handled under our Shipping & Returns policy.

Are the designs connected to a Buddhist organization?

No. Black Metal Buddha is an independent art and apparel project and does not represent a Buddhist lineage, temple, teacher, or institution.
