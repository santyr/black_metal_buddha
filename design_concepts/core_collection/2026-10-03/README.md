# Chöd — Offer Everything

Core Collection, Black Metal Buddha. Final Vajrayoginī pose revision (v3)
approved by the user on October 3, 2026. Built-in image generation was used.

The approved artwork uses a clothed contemporary interpretation of Vajrayoginī:
right leg extended, left knee bent, upward gaze, raised skull cup, lowered
curved knife, and a khatvāṅga staff. The Chöd offering theme and surrounding
spirit guests remain. This is apparel art, not a complete ritual diagram or
an assertion of lineage-specific iconographic accuracy. The robes and lotus
pedestal treatment are deliberate artistic adaptations. OFFER EVERYTHING is
original design copy, not an attributed quotation.

Revision history: initial seated Chöd practitioner; revised standing clothed
Vajrayoginī; final approved lunging pose. Only the approved source is in this
handoff. Production PNGs, two-ink SVG, proofs and printer instructions are in
black_metal_buddhist_prints under the chod_offer_everything slug.
