## Purpose and collection structure

Expand the approved Lineage Series with distinct portraits of Dzogchen teachers,
and establish a separate numbered collection, **Twelve Primordial Teachers**,
described in product copy as **The Twelve Dzogchen Buddhas**.

The Lineage Series follows teachers and transmission traditions; it is not a
single exhaustive succession chart. The Twelve collection follows the specific
traditional group culminating in Śākyamuni. Its cosmic timeframes belong to
sacred traditional history, not independently established historical chronology.
Garab Dorje and Longchenpa do not occupy slots in that group of twelve.

The Lightning Goats collaboration remains a separate series. Do not attach its
branding to these collections.

## Shared design system

Follow `docs/17_BRAND_GUIDELINES.md` and the approved Longchenpa/Garab Dorje art:

- Black garment and substantial negative space; bone #E9E1D5 carries most detail.
- Ritual red #971F1F is a restrained accent, not a bright full-background field.
- Distressed woodcut/engraving, bold name typography, readable lower caption.
- Distinct face, pose, silhouette and environment for each teacher. Do not reuse
  one fierce figure with different names or force peaceful teachers into wrath.
- No Christian crosses, cruciform ornaments, pentagrams, invented Tibetan text,
  or unrelated occult-symbol collage.
- Preserve documented teacher attributes when used. Record deliberate artistic
  departures. Do not present proposed captions as quotations or ritual texts.
- New collection footer: `TWELVE PRIMORDIAL TEACHERS` plus the fixed ordinal.
  Lineage footer remains `LINEAGE SERIES`.

## Briefs for the next three concepts

### 1. Mañjuśrīmitra — Beyond Concepts

Working slug: `manjushrimitra_beyond_concepts`  
Text: `MANJUSHRIMITRA` / `BEYOND CONCEPTS` / `LINEAGE SERIES`

An austere mature Indian scholar-yogin with a composed, penetrating expression.
A manuscript rests across his lap; its edges dissolve into fine smoke and open
space. A restrained red halo and low charnel-ground atmosphere carry the metal
mood. Keep the face and hands readable and avoid simply repeating Garab Dorje.

The manuscript dissolution and caption are original design metaphors. This
concept is not Mañjuśrī the bodhisattva: do not automatically add his sword or
other deity attributes. Longchenpa's account provides the teacher relationship
and arrangement of teachings; it does not prescribe this invented composition.

### 2. Yeshe Tsogyal — Wisdom Unbound

Working slug: `yeshe_tsogyal_wisdom_unbound`  
Text: `YESHE TSOGYAL` / `WISDOM UNBOUND` / `LINEAGE SERIES`

An authoritative adult Tibetan female teacher in draped robes, seated/kneeling
on a lotus before a dark cave and still lake. Flowing cloth and reflected halo
create a composition distinct from the male seated portraits. A curved ritual
knife and skull cup may follow the documented human/dakini representation
explained by Robert Beer. Avoid sexualized posing and generic fantasy armor.

Use the source for iconographic information, not as an image to copy. The cave,
lake treatment, palette and caption are original design decisions. Confirm
hands, implement shapes and overall identity at concept review.

### 3. VII — Drangsong Tröpé Gyalpo

Working slug: `drangsong_trope_gyalpo_wrathful_kingly_sage`  
Revised shirt text: `DRANGSONG TROPE GYALPO` / `WRATHFUL KINGLY SAGE` /
`VII` / `TWELVE PRIMORDIAL TEACHERS`

An imposing wrathful sage, forceful human-like face, compact powerful posture,
flame halo and dark rocky environment. Per the user’s revision, make the teacher’s name the dominant top title and
place the English epithet below the figure. Retain VII and the series footer.

Longchenpa's history identifies him teaching among rākṣasas. It does not supply
all anatomical details. Initial concept is explicitly interpretive. Himalayan
Art Resources item 52348070 and its linked complete initiation-card set are the
reference leads; the detailed image was not accessible in text retrieval.
Before production, visually verify a chosen reference or retain a clearly
labelled artistic treatment. Do not invent a canonical weapon, arm count,
crown or animal mount and claim it is sourced. Avoid portraying the recipients
as merely enemies being slaughtered.

## Production workflow and acceptance gates

1. **Research card:** identity, series, ordinal where applicable, reference URLs,
   known attributes, source uncertainty, and proposed artistic departures.
2. **Concept:** generate one distinct standalone artwork for each next design,
   using approved brand art only as the visual-style reference. Preserve the
   exact prompt, generated source and pending-approval status.
3. **Review:** show all three separately. User approves or requests changes to
   each. Do not infer that approval of this plan approves unseen artwork.
4. **Production preparation:** preserve approved composition, trace two-color
   vector if suitable, outline lettering, export transparent sRGB RGBA PNGs
   with 300 ppi metadata. Keep source-resolution limits explicit.
5. **Package:** standard 3600 × 4800 canvas, cropped proportional Printful upload,
   editable two-ink SVG, exact black-background proofs, approved concept/source,
   per-design printer note, metadata entry and updated SHA-256 checksums.
6. **Verification:** fully decode every PNG; check pixel size, alpha, profile,
   nominal DPI and bounds; inspect SVG for external fonts/embedded raster;
   compare proof to approved art, check spelling and religious-symbol guardrail,
   and run existing package tests. Confirm scoped changes before pushing.
7. **Provider setup:** confirm actual Comfort Colors 1717 size-specific print
   areas, position on each size and generate official Printful mockups. Start
   around 11 inches visible width, constrained by permitted height; never stretch.
   Assign real provider IDs, unique SKUs and approved prices in Printful.
8. **Sample and launch:** evaluate fine lettering, hatching, bone/red rendering,
   placement, garment hand and wash results. Sample approval precedes a larger
   run. Existing storefront sync imports saved Printful products; artwork files
   or documentation alone do not publish products or enable checkout.

Use existing `black_metal_buddhist_prints/01_transparent_png` through
`06_sources` conventions for approved artwork. Keep unapproved designs in
`design_concepts/dzogchen_series/2026-10-03/`, outside production specifications
and checksums. Record each as `concept_pending_approval`.
