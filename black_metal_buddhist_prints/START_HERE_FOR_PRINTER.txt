CANVAS, SIZE AND GARMENT
Production canvases are 12 x 16 inches (30.48 x 40.64 cm), 3600 x 4800
pixels. Use a BLACK garment. Transparent areas intentionally reveal garment
black. Scale proportionally; do not stretch artwork to fill a different
print area.

The actual garment model, garment size, printing process, and permitted
print area must be confirmed in Printful before production. Placement is not
a substitute for reviewing the exact artwork at output size.

RELIGIOUS-SYMBOL GUARDRAIL
Black Metal Buddha intentionally excludes Christian iconography and accidental
cross-like ornamentation. Do not add crosses, inverted crosses, cruciform
finials, church-cross silhouettes, or replacement flourishes that read as
Christian symbols during prepress.

If production cleanup is required, use neutral/Buddhist-compatible shapes
such as crescents, circles, wheel geometry, dots, lotus forms, vajra-like
ornament, or abstract thorn/sigil linework that does not form a cross.

REQUIRED BEFORE A RUN
Inspect all lettering, fine ornamental strokes, negative-space holes, and
distress at actual output size. The shop/provider may need to simplify tiny
islands or minimum gaps for its equipment. Obtain approval before making
material visual changes.

The printer/provider must determine underbase, color matching, trapping,
choke, print sequence, halftone behavior, and any other process-specific
prepress requirements.

Request/approve physical samples before a larger run. Physical approval must
include fine detail, red/bone reproduction, hand feel, placement, garment
fit, and post-wash behavior.
