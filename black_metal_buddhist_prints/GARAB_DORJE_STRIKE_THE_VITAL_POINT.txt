ICONOGRAPHY AND ATTRIBUTION
This is a contemporary artistic wrathful interpretation of Garab Dorje,
not a claim of canonical wrathful iconography. Traditional descriptions
consulted portray him as white and peaceful, including a teaching gesture.
The caption is thematic design copy, not presented as a verbatim quotation.
